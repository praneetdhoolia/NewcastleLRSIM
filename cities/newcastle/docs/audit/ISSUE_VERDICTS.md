## What changed the answers

Three verdicts moved off their issue bodies, all for the same reason the brief
predicted — the measurement frame, not the model:

1. **#30 was measured in a mismatched frame.** The audit compared distances of
   *all* core residents against the *Newcastle LGA* HTS rows. Newcastle is the
   densest LGA; its education trips are 3.0 km against Lake Macquarie's 7.2 and
   Port Stephens' 12.9. Compared like-for-like, most cells are within ±20%. What
   survives is specific: Newcastle education 1.86×, the Port Stephens
   shopping/other inflation that points at #32, and the sub-km trip scarcity
   that caps walk near 5%.
2. **#28's defect was already fixed in code** (the `CitysimControler` travel-time
   and disutility bindings), which no re-read of the config would show — the
   config alone still looks defective. What is open is the measured residual.
3. **#37's magnitude halved** when the demand builders were rewired onto their
   declared values and B2 was regenerated (commit `70ee585`). The old 0.66% was
   true of plans that no longer exist.

Everything structural (#20, #24, #29, #31, #32's state) re-confirmed — claims
about *absence* survive a model change unless something was added, and nothing
was.

## Measurement provenance

- Plan-pass statistics: single `iterparse` pass over
  `cities/newcastle/demand/plans/matsim/population_WEEKDAY.xml.gz` — person/leg totals
  reconcile exactly with `_plans_report.json` (524,125 / 2,255,047).
- Per-LGA stratification: `person_id` → `home_sa1` from
  `cities/newcastle/demand/population/B1_synthetic_population.csv`, `home_sa1` → LGA from
  `cities/newcastle/data/processed/zones/sa1_to_lga.csv`; HTS comparator is
  `JOURNEY_AVG_DISTANCE`, 2024/25 LGA rows of
  `cities/newcastle/data/processed/hts/hts_purpose.csv`, journey-weighted where a model
  purpose maps to several HTS purposes (other ← Social/recreation + Personal
  business + Other). That column remains a **constraint, not a target**.
- #34 geometry: seven street-name extents from
  `cities/newcastle/data/processed/network/A1_road_edges.csv` inside a ~4 km window
  around the box (the window is what exposed the Wallsend homonym), against the
  `cbd_buildings` extent in `cities/newcastle/geometry/analysis_extents.json`.
- Config claims: read from the emitted
  `cities/newcastle/scenarios/matsim/S2/WEEKDAY/config.xml` and
  `src/java/citysim/CitysimControler.java` / `src/run/run_matsim.py`.

**No holdout row was read.** No run was performed; nothing here is a model
output.
